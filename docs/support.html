Tajweed Practice – Support

Tajweed Practice is displayed as Tajweed beneath the app icon on your device and is developed by Ebaid LLC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change the reciter?

Go to Settings → Default Reciter and select from the available reciters.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings → Language and choose your preferred language. The app supports Arabic, English, Urdu, Turkish, French, Indonesian, German, and Spanish.

How do I bookmark an ayah?

Long-press on any ayah to bookmark it. Bookmarked ayahs are indicated with a gold bookmark icon.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Quran text is cached for reading, and you can download selected recitations and Tafseer from Settings. An internet connection is required for initial content retrieval and periodic content validation.

How do I share an Ayah, Tafseer, or tajweed rule?

Use the share button on the relevant screen. Shared content identifies Tajweed Practice and includes its App Store link.

What do the tajweed colors mean?

Tap on any colored word to see which tajweed rule applies. Each color represents a different recitation rule (ghunnah, idgham, ikhfa, etc.).

How do I remove my local data?

Delete individual bookmarks or downloaded content in the App where available. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored preferences, progress, bookmarks, and cached Quran content from the device.
