Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Tajweed ("the App") is developed by Ebaid LLC. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not send bookmarks, notes, reading history, selected reciters, or other user preferences to Quran.Foundation. These items are stored locally on your device.

Network Requests

The App makes network requests only when needed to provide Quran content, search, fonts, and audio:

  • Ebaid LLC Cloudflare Worker (tajweed-quran-proxy.ebaidllc.workers.dev and tajweed-quran-proxy-production.ebaidllc.workers.dev) — securely proxies Quran.Foundation Content API requests without exposing API credentials in the App
  • Quran.Foundation (apis.quran.foundation) — provides Quran text, word data, tajweed markup, translations, Tafseer, recitation metadata, and Content Sync updates through the Worker
  • Quran.com Search API (api.quran.com) — receives the search words a user enters while Search API approval and proxy migration are pending
  • Quran.Foundation CDN (verses.quran.com / audio.qurancdn.com) — provides audio recitation files
  • Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) — for Arabic font rendering

The App does not add names, email addresses, bookmarks, notes, or advertising identifiers to these requests. Like most internet services, the service operators may receive ordinary connection information such as an IP address, request headers, and timestamps under their own privacy practices.

Offline Quran Content

Quran text, translations, Tafseer, recitation metadata, and selected audio may be cached locally for offline reading and listening. When the device is online, the App periodically validates cached Quran content and applies Quran.Foundation updates, deletions, and invalidations. Cached content is used inside the App and is not exposed as a downloadable raw dataset.

Microphone Access

If enabled, microphone access is used exclusively for local recitation practice. Recordings remain on the device and are not uploaded by the current version of the App.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via the App Store listing or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
