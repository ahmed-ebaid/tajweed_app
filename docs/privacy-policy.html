Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Tajweed ("the App") is developed by Ebaid LLC. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All user preferences (bookmarks, last-read position, selected reciter) are stored locally on your device.

Network Requests

The App connects to the following third-party services solely to fetch Quran text, translations, and audio recitations:

  • Quran.com API (api.quran.com) — for Quran text, tajweed data, translations, and tafseer
  • Quran.com CDN (verses.quran.com / audio.qurancdn.com) — for audio recitation files
  • Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com) — for Arabic font rendering

No personal identifiers are sent with these requests.

Microphone Access

If enabled, microphone access is used exclusively for local recitation practice features. Audio is processed on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Analytics & Tracking

The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via the App Store listing or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
